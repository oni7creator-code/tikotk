Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how this application handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

This application accesses your social media accounts solely for the purpose of publishing content on your behalf. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data beyond what is necessary to operate the service.

2. Social Media Access

When you connect a social media account, we receive an access token from that platform. This token is used only to publish content you have authorized. We do not access your messages, contacts, or any other account data.

3. Data Storage

No personal information is stored on external servers. All data remains on your local machine.

4. Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or share your information with third parties. Social media platforms you connect to are governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the application constitutes acceptance of any changes.

7. Contact

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our GitHub repository.
